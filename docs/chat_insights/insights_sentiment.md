## Cross-cutting observations

**Confidence tracks pain, not just numbers.** The two deepest lows (Jan 2025, Jun 2026) were injury + life-stress compounds, and in both cases the turning point was a *painless session*, not a PR. "Painless" is his strongest positive keyword — worth treating as a first-class training KPI.

**Adherence is exceptional.** He logs nearly every session with video + RPE for 4.5 years and is candid about lapses (beer, poor sleep, missed secondary sessions). Gaps are almost always **external**: 3 travel trips (May–Jun 2024, Oct 2024 China, May–Jun 2025 Spain), illnesses, injuries, the 2025 job, and the 2026 Masters semester.

**Motivation drivers:** competition/rivalry (Yudhisthir, Ujjwol, Anmol at NYFC/Ox), explicit podium and total goals (600 → 650), coach reassurance ("tension na leu, will get it on meet day"), and post-injury painless sessions.

**Stress drivers:** work hours (2025 job), study load (2026 Masters), travel-induced detraining guilt, illness in peak weeks, and administrative frustration (missed meet registration).

**Resilience signature:** he repeatedly competes and PRs *through* adversity — fever at NYFC (still 8/9), gastritis then 615 total, back injury then registers Nationals. The coach's steady "be patient / strong is strong" framing is a consistent stabiliser during the dips.
