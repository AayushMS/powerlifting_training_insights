## Patterns & flags for the user

**1. Nerve symptoms in the hands/arms are the single most recurrent complaint** (May 2024, Aug–Sep 2024, Mar 2025, Nov 2025). They cluster around three triggers that repeat across years: **narrow deadlift grip**, **weighted pull-ups**, and **heavy squat bracing / cervical-spine load**. Coach's proven fixes: widen the DL grip, swap weighted pull-ups for lat pulldowns, straps on back-offs, neck warmups. → *Flag: worth a standing neuro/physio check of the cervical spine and a permanent grip-width + straps policy rather than re-diagnosing each flare.*

**2. The lower back is the highest-consequence recurring site — and it breaks in the weeks *after* peaks/meets.** Two 🔴 episodes: **Dec 2024–Jan 2025** (post-NYFC) and **June 2026** (post-comp / post blood donation). Both featured deadlift discomfort, weak/tight posterior chain, physio, and a forced deload/reset. Coach's own words (16/12/2024): *"after the peak the body is in a vulnerable state."* → *Flag: build a **structured post-meet re-introduction** (2–4 weeks of capped load + core correctives) into every plan instead of ramping straight back.*

**3. Illness reliably strikes peak weeks and meets.** COVID/fever pre-meet Jan 2022; **fever 102.8°F + food poisoning right before NYFC peak (Nov 2024)**; **fever 103.6°F at the NYFC meet itself (Dec 2024)**; gastritis/food poisoning June–July 2025. **Office-canteen food poisoning appears twice.** → *Flag: tighten food hygiene and immune support in the final 2–3 weeks of every prep; avoid canteen/outside food in peak week.*

**4. Left-sided asymmetry runs through everything** — left hip flexor (2022, 2026), left arm nerves (2024), left glute/lumbar (2025), left leg pointing out at squat bottom (2026). → *Flag: a dedicated left-side mobility/stability audit could pre-empt several of these.*

**5. Eyes (subconjunctival haemorrhage).** Both eyes burst in 2022; sensitivity and fear of recurrence resurfaced in 2024. Managed with hydration + eye drops. → *Flag: hydration and controlled bracing (avoid maximal breath-holds) matter beyond just comfort.*

**6. Recovery debt from work/study consistently precedes dips.** The 2025 9-to-6 job (15–16 hr days) and the 2026 Masters semester (3-consecutive-day training crunches) both directly preceded flares and performance drops. → *Flag: the schedule constraint is a first-class training variable — protect sleep and avoid back-to-back training days when externals spike.*

**Recurring management toolkit that has worked** (reuse these): physio + electric stim, corrective core work (planks/dead bug/dead hang/pelvic tilt), hip-opening mobility (pigeon, hip CARs, 90-90, hip aeroplane), straps on back-offs, wider DL grip, lat pulldown over weighted pull-ups, RPE-capped reload weeks, "don't take full time off — light primary + correctives."
