## The 7 highest-value insights

1. **"Painless" is his real leading indicator — not tonnage.** His two deepest motivation lows (Jan 2025, Jun 2026) were injury+life-stress compounds, and both turning points were a *pain-free session*, not a PR (28/01/2025 "jhandai birsisake thye"; 08/06/2026 "🥹"). Track pain-free sessions as a KPI.

2. **The lower back breaks *after* peaks, not during them.** Two 🔴 episodes — post-NYFC (Dec 2024–Jan 2025) and June 2026 — both hit in the vulnerable post-competition window the coach explicitly named ("after the peak the body is in a vulnerable state"). A structured post-meet re-entry would likely prevent the next one.

3. **Nerve symptoms in the hands/arms are the most *frequent* complaint** (2024×3, 2025, Nov 2025), and they trace to the same triggers every time: narrow deadlift grip, weighted pull-ups, and heavy squat bracing. The fixes already work (wider grip, straps on back-offs, lat pulldown instead of weighted pull-ups) — they just aren't yet a standing policy.

4. **Illness reliably strikes peak weeks.** COVID pre-meet (2022), fever+food poisoning pre-NYFC (Nov 2024), a 103.6°F fever *at* NYFC (Dec 2024), gastritis (2025). Office-canteen food poisoning appears twice. Final-prep food hygiene and immune load are a recurring failure point.

5. **Externals (work, study) are a first-class training variable.** The 2025 9-to-6 job (15–16 hr days) and the 2026 Masters semester (3-consecutive-day training crunches) each directly preceded flares and performance dips. When externals spike, sleep and non-back-to-back scheduling matter more than program tweaks.

6. **The deadlift is the engine of his progress** — 250 (2024) → 255 (2025) → 260/262.5 (late 2025) → **275** (2026). The single most-repeated coaching cue across 4.5 years is the deadlift **"pull the slack, wedge, then pull — be patient."**

7. **He plays smart in competition.** At Ox Classic 2025 he deliberately took conservative attempts to secure the podium ("plan B on every lift, I feel fresh") with more in the tank. Meet totals understate his true training ceiling by ~10–15 kg.

## How to use this chat data to your advantage

### For training decisions (you + coach)
- **Add a post-meet re-entry protocol.** Given the two post-peak back injuries, script the 2–4 weeks after every meet as capped-load + core correctives, not a straight ramp. The chat already contains the working recipe (physio + planks/dead bug/dead hang/pelvic tilt + light primary).
- **Make the nerve-symptom fixes permanent**, not per-flare: fixed wider DL grip, straps on all back-offs, lat pulldown in place of weighted pull-ups, and a standing cervical-spine warmup. Consider a one-time neuro/physio assessment.
- **Treat peak-week hygiene as programming.** No canteen/outside food, extra hydration and sleep in the final 2–3 weeks — illness has cost you a clean meet more than once.
- **Guard against back-to-back training days** when work/study is heavy — this exact pattern (3 consecutive days) preceded the Jan 2026 and Jun 2026 hip/back issues.
- **Reuse the "peak-early, balance fatigue" model** — it produced your best-ever prep (Ox Classic 2025).

### For the dashboard (chat + sheet → features)
- **Injury / illness overlay:** shade the injury and illness windows from `insights_injuries.md` onto the strength charts. The Jan 2025 and Jun 2026 dips instantly read as injuries, not deloads.
- **"Painless session" adherence metric:** parse the chat for pain/painless mentions per week and plot a recovery-confidence line alongside load.
- **Auto-flagged discrepancies:** surface the reconciliations in `insights_discrepancies.md` — the missing **07/02/2026 Iconic Clash deadlift meet (275)**, the **synthetic "630 total"** (relabel as theoretical; show best-actual 615/605), and the **missing Apr–Jun 2026 bench** data.
- **Competition timeline from chat:** the chat's timestamps are ground truth — use them to auto-correct the sheet's approximate week dates (which drift up to ~2 weeks).
- **Sentiment / motivation arc:** a per-quarter mood line (see `insights_sentiment.md`) overlaid with meets and injuries tells the "why" behind the numbers.
- **Cue reminders:** surface the per-lift cue cheat-sheet (`insights_coaching_cues.md`) as pre-session reminders keyed to the day's main lift.
- **RPE-vs-load context:** the chat has RPE + bodyweight annotations the sheet lacks (BW ~85 kg in 2022 → 91–93 kg in 2025–26) — good candidates to backfill.
