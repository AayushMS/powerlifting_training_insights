## ⚙️ GENERAL / PROGRAMMING

- **"Everything is RPE" — hold the prescribed RPE regardless of the load** (16/12/2021 "Everything is RPE 😂"; even set RPE 6–7 caps on basketball).
- **Secondary days = nail technique + rehearse the primary lift** (21/03/2025).
- **Diagnose from warm-ups; film multiple angles; change ONE thing at a time** (12/08/2025).
- **Meet-day: take 15 kg jumps (not 20); "the first rep is what counts"; pick attempts off the last warm-up** (29/11/2024; 21/11/2024; 04/10/2025).
- **"Peak early and balance the fatigue to build confidence"** — the philosophy behind his best 2025 prep (28/04/2025).
- **Don't train slow — "the meet is faster than training"** (01/03/2025).

## 🛌 RECOVERY

- **Hip-opening mobility on rest days**: pigeon stretch, hip CARs, glute bridge, lateral lunges, 90-90, child's pose, hip aeroplane (05/01/2022; 11/04/2026).
- **After a peak, "the body is in a vulnerable state" — be careful with external factors** (16/12/2024).
- **On injury, DON'T take complete time off — light primary movement + corrective exercises + stay active** (11/01/2025).
- **Core activation for the back**: 3-way plank, dead bug, dead hang, pelvic tilt — "active core = less stress on the back" (02/06/2026).
- **Pre-workout activation: glutes, hams, lower back** (13/06/2026).
- **Recovery & sleep are a priority; don't overdo extra activity — "manageable hunu paryo"** (01/03/2025; 28/06/2026).

## 🥗 NUTRITION

- **Save daily energy expenditure for training** — cap recreational sport at RPE 6–7 (26/12/2021).
- **Dial in nutrition + sleep from ~1 week out** (04/02/2022).
- **Alcohol: "occasionally is fine, but every weekend?"** (23/03/2024).
- **"Nutrition is better than runs" for your goals** — steered him away from adding cardio (27/06/2026).

## 🧠 MINDSET

- **"Be patient."** The through-line of the entire relationship — appears in squat, deadlift, injury, and prep contexts.
- **"Strong is strong mentality"** / **"God is spotter"** on PR attempts (31/07/2025; 05/10/2025).
- **"Treat every rep, every set as an attempt to PR"** (04/01/2022).
- **Meet day: "you've got to execute in one go — you won't get a second chance"** (10/03/2025).
- **Reassurance in dips: "tension na leu, will get it on meet day"** (03/02/2022).

### The 6 cues Ojash repeats most (if you remember nothing else)
1. **Deadlift:** pull the slack, wedge, *then* pull — be patient.
2. **Squat:** stay on mid-foot; patience out of the hole; fight your knees.
3. **Bench:** dead-stop on the chest, straight bar path, load both elbows equally.
4. **All lifts:** hold the prescribed RPE — "everything is RPE."
5. **Recovery:** don't fully rest an injury — light primary + correctives; respect the post-peak vulnerable window.
6. **Mindset:** be patient; strong is strong; the first rep is what counts.
